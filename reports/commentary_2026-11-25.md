# Match notes — 25 November 2026 (R14, second night)

Model numbers are the pregame forecasts; "expected line" is model + 5.63, the
measured shade. League context for 2026-27: **64.0 c-reb**, 69.9 missed FG,
151.5 total possessions per game.

The "history" number below is opponent-adjusted: a team's own rate averaged
with what this opponent concedes, so it is not just a raw average.

## 19:00 — DUB-PRT (single)

**Model 62.5 · expected line ~68.1 · history ~65.4**

- Pace is the quiet part: Dubai's home games run 145.6 possessions and
  Partizan's road games 147.4, against a 151.5 league average. That is 4-6
  fewer possessions, worth roughly 3 fewer c-reb before anything else.
- Dubai at home grabs 36.3 c-reb (offensive rate 0.279, a top-five figure),
  and Partizan concedes 35.2 on the road — so Dubai's own number projects
  around 36.
- Partizan on the road: 31.0 c-reb, and only 9.2 offensive boards a game
  (rate 0.243). Weak second-chance volume is what keeps the total down.
- Last season's two meetings: 59 and 60.
- **The risk:** Dubai's glass work. If their bigs repeat 36+ and Partizan
  adds 32-33, the game lands at 68-69 and a 67 line is a loss. Overtime with
  two fast teams is the other way it goes wrong.

**Expectation:** a 63-66 game, most likely 64-65. Under needs the total to
stay below the line by 3-5, which is the normal shade, not a gift. This is
the thinnest of the week — play it at 66 or above, comfortable at 68.

- **The risk:** both drivers compounding — Žalgiris's glass rate at Madrid's

**Verdict:** the anchor leg. Widest margin under either reading.

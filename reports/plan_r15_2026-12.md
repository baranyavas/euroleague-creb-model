## Decision rules (unchanged)

1. Line opens 60 minutes before that game's own tip-off.
2. Cushion = line − model. **≥ +5** combo leg, **+3…+5** single only, **< +3** pass.
3. A leg that does not open, or opens too low, drops out. The ticket shrinks;
   it is never topped up with a leg from another window.
4. No live legs, ever. Everything is placed before the first tip in the window.
5. Maximum two tickets a night, flat 65 on Empire + 65 on Duel each.
6. Nothing in this file is a forecast made after a result — the numbers are
   frozen here before the lines exist.

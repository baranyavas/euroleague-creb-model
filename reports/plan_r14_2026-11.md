## 24 November — one ticket, chosen by the MUN-TEL line

The user skips the 20:00 and 21:00 games (Paris, Virtus). Both are singles-only
anyway: 60 minutes apart, outside the 45-minute window.

Two windows share PAM-PAN and ASV-MIL, so only **one** ticket belongs on this
night, not two.

- **Window A (22:00-22:45):** MUN-TEL + PAM-PAN + ASV-MIL
- **Window B (22:30-23:00):** PAM-PAN + ASV-MIL + BAR-ULK

**The gate is MUN-TEL's gap (`model - line`):**

| MUN-TEL gap | decision |
|---|---|
| **>= +5** | Window A: the 3-fold including MUN-TEL |
| **+3 to +5** | Drop MUN-TEL (thin band is single-only, never a combo leg) -> Window B |
| **< +3** | Pass on MUN-TEL -> Window B |

In Window B each leg must clear +5 on its own; any leg under +5 drops out and
the ticket shrinks to a 2-fold rather than absorbing a thin leg.

## Sanity check on the ticket shapes

At the historical +5 shade, an under leg clears ~77% of the time; a 3-fold pays
about 6.33 and carries ~46%, EV ~+189%. A 2-fold pays ~3.42 at ~59%, EV ~+103%.
That is the whole case for the 4-fold preference — 4 legs pay ~11.7 at ~35%,
EV ~+312% — and R14 cannot supply four legs. First 4-fold night is 15 Dec.

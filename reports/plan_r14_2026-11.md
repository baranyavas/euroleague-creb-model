anyway: 60 minutes apart, outside the 45-minute window.

night, not two.

- **Window A (22:00-22:45):** BAY-MAC + VAL-PAN + ASV-MIL
- **Window B (22:30-23:00):** VAL-PAN + ASV-MIL + BAR-FEN

**The gate is BAY-MAC's gap (`model - line`):**

| BAY-MAC gap | decision |
|---|---|
| **>= +5** | Window A: the 3-fold including BAY-MAC |
| **+3 to +5** | Drop BAY-MAC (thin band is single-only, never a combo leg) -> Window B |
| **< +3** | Pass on BAY-MAC -> Window B |

In Window B each leg must clear +5 on its own; any leg under +5 drops out and
the ticket shrinks to a 2-fold rather than absorbing a thin leg.
